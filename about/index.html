About

St. Acutis is made in honor of Saint Carlo Acutis.

Carlo Acutis (1991–2006) was an Italian teenager who loved computers and used what he knew to share good things online. Among other things, he built a website cataloguing Eucharistic miracles from around the world. He died at fifteen, and on 7 September 2025 he was canonized by the Catholic Church.

The name is simply “Saint Acutis”, abbreviated: St. Acutis. The site lives at stacutis.com.

The idea here is simple and borrowed from him: make useful tech, give it away for free, and hope it does some good for others. It's a side project by Victor Soares, a software developer from Brazil.
